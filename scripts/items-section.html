Items

Every obtainable item in this build and where it comes from — 123 sold in shops, 59 carried by wild Pokémon, and all 58 TMs & HMs with the move each one teaches. Shops in the ROM aren't tied to town names, so each is labelled by its counter type (Poké Mart, Dept. Store stone counter, battle-item stand, and so on).

◆Reading the shops

A “Sold at” tag names the kind of counter that stocks the item (the ROM stores shop inventories, not the town they sit in). Cross-reference the walkthrough above to place them on the map — a “Vitamins” counter is the Goldenrod/Celadon Dept. Store, an “Evolution stones” counter is the department-store stone floor, and so on.
