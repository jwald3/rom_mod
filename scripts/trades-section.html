In-Game Trades

Hand a trade partner the Pokémon they ask for and you walk away with something rarer, already holding an item, and carrying flawless 31 IVs in every stat — the best-statted Pokémon you can get without breeding. Traded Pokémon also earn bonus experience and obey past your badge cap. This build's trade table holds 13 records; 4 of them have a partner standing on a map you'll actually visit.

◆Read from the ROM

These entries come straight out of this build's gIngameTrades table — nickname, original trainer, held item, IVs, and the species asked for and given. The town comes from the NPC script that triggers each trade, so the 4 live entries below are the ones a script actually hooks up on a Johto or Kanto map.
